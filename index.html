🧬 EndoGlia Seminar Series

Hosted by the Barreto Lab, University of Limerick

EndoGlia is a seminar series hosted by the Barreto Lab at the University of Limerick, bringing together researchers working at the intersection of endocrinology, glial biology, and brain aging. Each session features invited speakers from across these fields, exploring how hormonal signals shape glial cell function, and what happens when that relationship breaks down in neurodegeneration and aging.
